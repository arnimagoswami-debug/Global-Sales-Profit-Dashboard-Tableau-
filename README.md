# 🌍 Global Sales & Profit Dashboard (Tableau)

## 📊 Overview
This interactive **Tableau dashboard** provides a comprehensive analysis of global sales, profit, orders, and discount impact.  
It helps understand business performance by region, category, and customer segment.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Total Sales:** $12.6M  
- **Total Profit:** $1.47M  
- **Total Orders:** 51.3K  
- **Average Order Value:** 505  
- **Return Rate:** 4.7%

---

## 🧩 Dashboard Highlights
- **Category-wise Profit Contribution:** Pie chart showing profit distribution across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.  
- **Sales Distribution by Sub-Category:** Bar chart visualizing sales performance across product sub-categories.  
- **Discount Impact on Profit:** Bubble chart analyzing how discounts affect profitability.  
- **Top 5 Countries by Sales:** Map-style grid showing leading countries by sales and profit.  
- **Monthly Sales & Profit Trend:** Line chart showing monthly growth patterns.  
- **Top 10 Customers by Sales:** Bar chart highlighting top customers driving the most revenue.  
- **Interactive Filters:** Region, Segment, Category, and Month filters for dynamic analysis.

---

## 🧠 Tools & Technologies
- **Tool:** Tableau Desktop  
- **Data Source:** Excel (sample data)  
- **Visualization Type:** Interactive KPI dashboard  
- **Metrics:** Sales, Profit, Orders, Discounts, Return Rate
